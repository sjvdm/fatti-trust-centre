## How to use this trust centre

Use these pages for an initial review. Fatti can answer a client questionnaire against the applicable service scope and can provide approved evidence through an appropriate confidential review process. Public summaries are deliberately less detailed than operational and security records.

This site is versioned in Git. Published changes pass through a controlled review process and the public repository's protected branch.

For security, privacy or technical assurance queries, contact [sj@fatti.co.za](mailto:sj@fatti.co.za) or [jaco@fatti.co.za](mailto:jaco@fatti.co.za).
